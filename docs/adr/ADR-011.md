# ADR-011: Empirical Evaluation Methodology & Claims Policy

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Academic and professional integrity requires that all AI accuracy, deflection, and latency numbers be empirically measured rather than copied from external papers.

## Considered Options
1. Quoting published benchmarks from research literature
2. Automated local evaluation suite with versioned datasets and bootstrap confidence intervals

## Decision
Adopt **Automated Evaluation Suite (/ml/eval) with 95% Bootstrap Confidence Intervals**.
- Evaluation runner measures Macro-F1, PR-AUC, precision@1, and latency.
- Dataset SHA-256 hashes committed to version control.
- Strict claims policy: only numbers output by `/ml/eval/results/metrics.json` may be cited in reports.
